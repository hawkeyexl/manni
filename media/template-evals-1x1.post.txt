A page that calls itself a how-to makes a promise. Something should hold it to that promise.

manni lint templates can now carry evals. manni docevals grades them on every page lint routes to that template by its type.

The built-in templates from The Good Docs Project now ship TGDP's own guidance as evals. A how-to page gets six of them. They check that it names the task, starts each step with a verb and shows the result, among others.

Your house template can extend the built-in, skip an eval that doesn't fit, and add its own. In the video, a house eval checks that a how-to shows the manni command it documents. The page doesn't, so it fails on the line that declares its type, and the run exits 1.

Every frame is a real run against a small test project.
