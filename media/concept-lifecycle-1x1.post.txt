Your pages tag concepts freely. Your glossary covers half of them. And the questions your docs promise to answer are written down nowhere a tool can check.

manni term check now fails on a concept no term page defines. manni term fill creates those pages. With a model it drafts the definitions, and with --offline nothing leaves the machine.

The graph takes its concept scheme from that termbase, so one concept is one node, however a page spells it.

Each page can declare the questions it answers. manni graph fill pins each question to the lines that answer it, here with a 3B model running locally on CPU.

Change those lines and manni graph check says so: that answer is no longer where the question was pinned.
