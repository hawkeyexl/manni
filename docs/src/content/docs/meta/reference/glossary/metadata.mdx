<p>{frontmatter.definition}</p>

{/* cite metadata-is-generic-key-values */}
They are fields such as `type`, `title`, and `timestamp`. Metadata is what an
[extractor](/manni/meta/reference/glossary/extractor/) pulls out of a
document's [frontmatter](/manni/meta/reference/glossary/frontmatter/).
Everything in manni meta after extraction operates on this generic key/value
shape, regardless of the original format. manni meta checks the **presence and
format** of metadata, never the document's prose.
