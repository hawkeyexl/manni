<p>{frontmatter.definition}</p>

A term lists its mappings in `exact-matches`, `close-matches`,
`broad-matches`, `narrow-matches` and `related-matches`. Each becomes the
SKOS mapping property of that kind. `manni term check` reports an IRI listed
under two kinds, and `manni graph check` reports a mapping that points into
the scheme itself. See [Check rules](/manni/term/reference/rules/#conflicting-matches).
