title: Rest each persona on evidence that still holds
description: Each persona's goals and pains, and each audience's claims, are pinned to the evidence they rest on, and a pin whose evidence changed fails the check.
personas: [sara]
---

**Outcome.** Every persona and audience rests on at least one pin, and each pinned goal or pain names its evidence by pointer. A record with no evidence fails `manni meta validate`. A pin whose evidence changed or vanished fails `manni cite check`. "Which of Theo's pains rest on anything?" is one command.

**Steps.** Sara's trigger is a persona that passes every check while nothing ties its pains to what they rest on. She moves each goal and pain into the persona's `goals:` and `pains:` fields, one claim per item. She gives the `strategy` collection a `{page}.citations.yaml` manifest, and names `manni:citations:1.3.0` on the audience and persona overrides. She adds `citations` to `required` in each record schema, so a record with no pin fails `meta validate`. She pins a pain with `manni cite add theo.md#/pains/0 <evidence>:<line>`. A line of a record's body still takes `theo.md:<line>`, and a frontmatter line is refused with the pointer to use instead. The evidence is a tracked file in the repository, a private checkout under `cite.repos`, or an export folder with no git. `manni cite check` reads each pin `current`. A reordered list reads `claim-moved`, and `manni cite update` rewrites the pointer. A reworded pain reads `claim-changed`, and a deleted one reads `claim-missing`. Edited evidence reads `source-changed` and vanished evidence `source-missing`, and both fail the run.

**What success looks like.** Every record names what it rests on, and the check holds it there on every push. A pain nobody re-tested reads as a claim, not as a finding. Related journeys are S17 (the records) and S3 (the baseline ratchet).
