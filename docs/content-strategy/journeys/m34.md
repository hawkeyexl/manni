**Outcome.** Maya decides whether manni solves a problem her docset has, before she installs anything. If it does, she knows which tool to try first.

**Steps.** Her trigger is a link from a colleague or a channel. She has not chosen a tool yet, and every other journey assumes she has. She reads the home page. It says what manni does in plain words, then lists the ways documentation breaks quietly and which tool catches each one. She finds the failure her own docset has. A page went stale after a code change, or search dropped a page with a missing field. She opens that tool's overview, which starts with the same failure in more detail and places the tool among the others. Then she validates one file to see the check work on her own content.

**What success looks like.** Maya can say in one sentence what manni would catch in her docset. Or she can say it catches nothing she needs, without reading past the home page to find out.
