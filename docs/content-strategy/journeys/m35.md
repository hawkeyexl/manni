**Outcome.** Maya sets up manni in the order its domains depend on each other. She takes only the tracks her docset needs, and one CI step gates all of them.

**Steps.** Her trigger is the home page after M34. She knows manni fits, and twelve tools each offer a first step. She walks the trunk first. She installs the package, declares one collection every tool reads, and holds the pages to a schema. Then she picks a track. Structure holds pages to templates, then grades what a template's pages must say. Provenance pins sentences to source, then encrypts the paths it pins. Vocabulary defines terms, then records who each page serves, then builds a graph from both. Published site builds the site, then checks its accessibility and its procedures. Each track starts from the trunk's end state, so she can take them in any order or skip one. After each step she runs `manni status` and sees what it put in play. Every track ends at the join. She replaces her pipeline's steps with one `manni check`, and installs the plugin so an agent hears about a broken page at the edit.

**What success looks like.** Maya never meets a step that needs something she has not set up. `manni status` shows exactly the tracks she took, and `manni check` in CI gates them.
