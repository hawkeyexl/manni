title: Watch a persona attempt a task before a reader does
description: A synthetic user plays one of Sara's personas, attempts a task from the docs, and tells her where the pages lost it.
personas: [sara]
---

**Outcome.** Sara learns whether a persona can finish a job with the pages, before a real reader tries. The gaps arrive as findings, with the persona's own account of where it got stuck. The tasks worth keeping become journeys with curated evals, the golden set that reruns on demand.

**Steps.** Sara's trigger is a journey she walked herself, as the author who already knows the product. She asks the question in one line instead, with `manni test tasks <page> --task "<what the reader wants>" --persona theo`. `--workspace` names a small repository for the persona to start in. manni starts Claude Code headless in a fresh temporary copy, tells it to play Theo, and points it at a copy of the docs. She needs no evals up front, because a write-in task is judged on completion and adherence. `completed` asks whether Theo did the task, and `followed-the-docs` whether every step came from the docs. One `constraint` warning asks about each constraint in Theo's record. She reads the account first, because it says in Theo's voice which page helped and which one sent him elsewhere. She moves Theo's goals, pains and constraints out of his record's body into fields. The synthetic user is told the fields, never the body. She fixes the page the account named and runs the same command until every eval passes. Then she keeps the task as a journey. She writes `evals:` that say what done means, beside `trigger`, `entry-point`, `success-criteria` and `workspace`, and makes the ones she can `command` or `regex` graders. From then on `manni test tasks --journey <id>` runs that journey, and a bare `manni test tasks` runs every journey that declares `evals:`.

**What success looks like.** The journey walk-through test's first step runs without her, from one line of plain text. A page that loses its reader fails with that reader's account of why. The journeys with evals are a golden set she reruns before a release. Related journeys are S17 (the records), S8 (assertions a judge can decide) and T1 (the journey a synthetic Theo walks).
